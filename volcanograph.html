🌋 System Mechanics

Magma forms beneath Earth’s surface due to intense heat and pressure. Before an eruption, scientists often observe key warning signs as magma pushes upward:

  • 📳 Increased Earthquakes: Rock breaking as magma moves.
  • ⛰️ Ground Deformation: The surface bulging outward.
  • 💨 Rising Gas Emissions: Magma releasing trapped gases as it nears the surface.
