🌋 System Mechanics

Magma forms beneath Earth’s surface due to intense heat and pressure. Before an eruption, scientists often observe key warning signs as magma pushes upward:

  • 1 Earthquakes: Rock breaking as magma moves.
  • 2 Ground Deformation: The surface bulging outward.
  • 3 Gas Emissions: Magma releasing trapped gases as it gets closer to the surface.
