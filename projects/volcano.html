Each group (2–3 students) turns in the following four parts:

  1. Guided Research Packet (your evidence + notes)
  2. Volcano Model (shows internal structure; eruption optional)
  3. 5–7 Minute Presentation (teach the class; everyone speaks)
  4. Student Worksheet + Answer Key (class completes during your talk)

Non-negotiables

  • Accuracy beats decoration.
  • Slides support your speaking — do not read slides.
  • Your worksheet must be approved before presentation day.

Work Days (3)

  • Day 1: Research Sections 1–3 + start slide outline
  • Day 2: Research Sections 4–7 + start model plan
  • Day 3: Build model + finalize slides + build worksheet + answer key

Presentation Days (2)

  • 5–7 minutes per group
  • Class completes your worksheet during/after your talk
  • Short Q&A: be ready to defend your claims
