🌍 The Ground Beneath

You are a geo-consulting team hired to analyze the ground in a specific region. Your client needs to understand the soil before investing in food production or infrastructure.

Driving Question

How does the geology of your region shape food, lifestyle, and safety for the people who live there?

  • 👉 Start by checking your Region Snapshot.
  • 👉 Then complete the Research Tasks.
  • 👉 Finish by building a clean, visual report (Slides or PDF).

What you turn in

  • A digital report (Slides or PDF)
  • Includes maps/visuals and a sources slide
  • Uses evidence: soil order, climate, crops, hazards, building constraints

Citations expectation

Use at least 2 credible sources and list them at the end. Avoid random blogs. Use data sources when possible.

Quick Profile

What you should find (minimum)

    Research Tasks

    1) Geology (What)

    • Confirm the soil order and describe composition
    • CLORPT: identify the dominant formation factor
    • Describe the soil profile/horizons and color causes

    2) Harvest (Food)

    • What grows well and why?
    • Soil deficiencies (nutrients, drainage, pH, etc.)
    • Create a “regional dinner plate” from realistic foods

    3) Infrastructure (Build)

    • Stability risks (shrink-swell, erosion, liquefaction, etc.)
    • How people build (materials + traditional methods)
    • Hazards: landslides, sinkholes, desertification, etc.

    Report requirement

    Your report must include a map, at least 3 visuals (photos/diagrams), and a sources slide.

    Resources

    Recommended sources

    • USDA Web Soil Survey (great for US regions)
    • ISRIC World Soil Information (global soil data)
    • SoilWeb Earth (UC Davis) (maps + profiles)

    If you use Wikipedia to start, you must verify with a stronger source.

    Grading Rubric (100 points)

    Optional Score Estimator

    Choose a performance level per category to estimate your score. (Teacher grade is final.)

    Estimated Score: 100 / 100

    Rubric details

    Category Points Exceeds / Meets / Developing / Beginning
    A. Geology Accuracy 25
    Exceeds (25): Correct soil order + strong CLORPT explanation + horizon/profile detail with evidence.
    Meets (20): Correct soil order + basic CLORPT + some profile detail.
    Developing (15): Partial accuracy, vague explanation, or missing key terms.
    Beginning (10): Major inaccuracies or missing major concepts.
    B. Agricultural Analysis 25
    Exceeds (25): Connects soil properties to crops with clear “why,” includes realistic dinner plate.
    Meets (20): Lists crops and gives basic reasons; dinner plate mostly realistic.
    Developing (15): Generic crops or weak connection to soil; dinner plate unrealistic.
    Beginning (10): Missing or incorrect crop/soil relationship.
    C. Infrastructure + Hazards 25
    Exceeds (25): Strong hazard analysis + real building methods linked to soil constraints.
    Meets (20): Mentions hazards + materials with some links to soil.
    Developing (15): Vague hazards/materials; little soil connection.
    Beginning (10): Missing or incorrect hazard/build info.
    D. Visuals + Professionalism 25
    Exceeds (25): Clean design, strong visuals/maps, accurate captions, sources correct.
    Meets (20): Organized report with visuals and mostly correct citations.
    Developing (15): Disorganized or text-heavy, weak visuals or missing citations.
    Beginning (10): Hard to read, no visuals, no sources.
